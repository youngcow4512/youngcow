YOUNGCOW — GITHUB PAGES

1. Extract this ZIP.
2. Upload ALL extracted files and the assets folder to the ROOT of your youngcow.github.io repository.
3. GitHub: Settings > Pages > Deploy from a branch > main > /(root) > Save.
4. Wait a few minutes and open https://youngcow.github.io/

Do not upload the ZIP itself as the only repository file. Upload the files inside it.
